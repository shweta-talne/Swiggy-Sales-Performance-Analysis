# 🚴 Swiggy Food Delivery App: Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end retail business intelligence project developed in **Microsoft Excel** to evaluate regional sales performance, consumer ratings, and volume metrics representing ₹53.01M in revenue data.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project centers around an interactive, professionally branded corporate dashboard designed to provide operational stakeholders with diagnostic visibility into sales trends across dynamic granularities (daily, weekly, monthly), geographic distribution (state and city layers), and product types.

## 📊 Core Performance Metrics Evaluated
* **Total Sales Volume:** ₹53.01 Million Gross Revenue
* **Average Delivery Rating:** 4.34 Out of 5 Stars
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹268.51
* **Total Transacted Orders:** 197.43K Deliveries

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation Details
* **Advanced Visual Architecture:** Deployed interactive geometric map visualizations alongside coordinated dual-axis trend lines, categorical donut profiles, and matrix style cards.
* **Interactive Filtering Slicers:** Integrated multi-tier chronological filters (Months, Weeks) alongside operational dimensions (Food Categories, Restaurant Names) for seamless stakeholder exploration.
* **UI/UX Custom Layout:** Styled entirely using Swiggy's corporate orange visual identity scheme, utilizing uniform rounded borders and optimized typography layout hierarchy to deliver an executive-level reporting interface.

## 💡 Key Business Insights Discovered
* **Dietary Preferences:** *Vegetarian* options command the vast majority of consumer choice, representing 64% of total volume (₹34.0M) compared to *Non-Veg* variants (36% at ₹19.0M).
* **Geographic Powerhouses:** Bengaluru stands out as the single highest revenue-generating city layout, contributing ₹5.46M in gross sales.
* **Weekly Purchasing Behavior:** Weekly distributions exhibit strong operational consistency, while daily metrics peak over late-week time frames (Thursday through Saturday clusters).
